ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Beyond
the Page.

Beyond the Page is an independent proof of concept exploring how AI could turn a conventional corporate website into a personalized digital experience.

Built with limited resources, it uses VANTER, a fictional engineering company, a curated demonstration knowledge base and a lightweight generation workflow. It is not a production-ready product. The aim is to demonstrate the approach, not to claim enterprise-grade capabilities.

VANTER and its products are fictional. No customer claims, commercial outcomes or product certifications are implied.

Prototype notice. Live AI generation uses a free-tier service with rate limits. During high demand, generation may be temporarily unavailable and a sample page will appear instead. Please try again later.

Let op: prototype. De AI-generator gebruikt een gratis dienst met gebruikslimieten. Bij drukte kan de generatie tijdelijk niet beschikbaar zijn en verschijnt er een voorbeeldpagina. Probeer het later opnieuw.

Ограничения прототипа. Генерация страниц использует бесплатный AI-сервис с лимитами запросов. При превышении лимита генерация может быть временно недоступна: вместо неё появится демонстрационная страница. Повторите попытку позже.
