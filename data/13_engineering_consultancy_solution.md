# Engineering consultancy solution

> Fictional demonstration content. Not a real vendor claim.

Engineering consultancies often need cross-discipline document control, controlled reviews and transparent handover to clients. DesignHub is the primary VANTER module for this audience; Connect may support exports to client systems.

An illustrative workflow includes creating a document register, assigning review owners, tracking approval states and producing a controlled handover package. Where the consultancy also manages site coordination, ProjectFlow may be relevant.

Evaluation questions: Which document numbering rules are used? What review gates are mandatory? Do clients require IFC metadata or only approved PDFs? What is the expected volume of external reviewers? Is a client-specific export format needed?

DesignHub does not author engineering calculations or replace discipline-specific CAD tools. A consultancy should validate compatibility with its actual toolchain before procurement.
