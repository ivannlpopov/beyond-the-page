# EPC contractor solution

> Fictional demonstration content. Not a real vendor claim.

EPC contractors must coordinate design packages, subcontractors, changes and construction progress across disciplines. VANTER's proposed combination is DesignHub for engineering records, ProjectFlow for work packages and change coordination, and Connect for exchanging approved metadata.

Illustrative journey: a design manager publishes a revised drawing in DesignHub; the project engineer links it to a change request; ProjectFlow identifies affected work packages; designated reviewers assess impacts; the team closes the issue with a documented decision. This is an illustrative workflow, not an automated guarantee of impact detection.

Typical evaluation questions: How are revisions approved? Which parties need external access? What is the source of the schedule baseline? How are contractor identifiers mapped? Which reports are required for the client?

Do not promise automatic prevention of delays or elimination of rework. A pilot should test a specific work-package and revision-control scenario with representative documents.
