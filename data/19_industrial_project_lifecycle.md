# Industrial project lifecycle and information needs

An industrial capital project can progress through investment decision, feasibility and concept, permitting and design development, detailed design, procurement and construction, commissioning, and operation. The exact stages and responsibilities depend on the contract and jurisdiction. The SEVERIN reference presentation maps responsibilities across these phases; VANTER uses this as **domain inspiration**, not a claim about SEVERIN software.

At feasibility stage, owners need scope definition, budget assumptions, risks and schedule alternatives. During design, engineering teams need controlled revisions, discipline coordination and issue tracking. During construction, managers need package status, decisions, site issues and evidence. At handover, owners need reliable as-built documents, equipment registers and operating records.

VANTER mapping (fictional): DesignHub manages document/revision metadata; ProjectFlow manages work packages and decisions; Connect handles assessed data exchanges; AssetTwin organises selected handover asset records. No VANTER module independently supplies legal approvals, engineering sign-off, or a complete project-delivery service.

Source boundary: the lifecycle and service categories are grounded in SEVERIN DEVELOPMENT's presentation. Product mapping is newly invented for the VANTER demo.
