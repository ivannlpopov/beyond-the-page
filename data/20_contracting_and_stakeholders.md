# EPC, EPCM and stakeholder responsibilities

EPC stands for Engineering, Procurement and Construction; EPCM stands for Engineering, Procurement and Construction Management. The SEVERIN presentation distinguishes a general construction contracting component within EPC from procurement/tender and construction-management functions within EPCM. The exact allocation of liability and procurement contracts must always be verified against project contracts.

Typical stakeholders and information needs:
- Asset owner / investor: programme exposure, investment assumptions, decision visibility, commissioning and handover readiness.
- Project director: schedule dependencies, package status, scope changes and accountability.
- Design manager / engineer: document revisions, IFC metadata, technical review decisions and interfaces.
- Construction manager: site issues, contractor coordination, approvals and close-out evidence.
- Procurement / commercial: supplier scope, licensing, implementation effort, support and auditability.
- IT / security: identity, deployment options, integrations, data ownership and security limitations.

An AI guide should ask about the **task**, not infer it from title alone. If asked for contractual responsibility, it should explain that the answer depends on the agreement and direct users to legal/project specialists.
