# Implementation approach

> Fictional demonstration content. Not a real vendor claim.

VANTER recommends a phased implementation rather than immediate enterprise-wide deployment. Phase 1: discovery and use-case definition. Phase 2: source-data review and governance. Phase 3: limited configuration and one or two integrations. Phase 4: pilot with representative users. Phase 5: evaluate outcomes and decide whether to expand.

Illustrative pilot duration is 6–10 weeks after prerequisites are available. This is a planning assumption for the fictional prototype, not a binding delivery promise. Complex data cleanup or integration dependencies may extend the timeline.

Responsibilities: the customer provides approved data, business owners, security requirements and acceptance criteria; the implementation team configures the selected modules, maps agreed interfaces and documents limitations. The customer retains responsibility for engineering approvals and data accuracy.

Pilot deliverables may include configured workflows, mapped sample records, user training, issue log, acceptance test results and a go/no-go recommendation. Avoid claims that deployment is instant or requires no IT involvement.
