# Support, escalation and AI response policy

> Fictional demonstration content. Not a real vendor claim.

For this fictional prototype, standard support channels are an in-app support request and a customer success contact assigned during onboarding. No phone number, email address, office address beyond Rotterdam, or support portal URL is defined. The AI must not invent them.

Incident categories: access issue; data import problem; integration error; product defect; enhancement request; and security concern. Severity definitions, response-time targets and support hours are not specified and require a service agreement.

Escalate to a human when a visitor requests pricing, contractual commitments, security attestations, unsupported integrations, safety-critical recommendations, or information that is missing or contradictory. Ask for the minimum information needed to route the request; do not request confidential project documents in an anonymous public session.

AI behavior: identify the visitor's objective; retrieve relevant approved sources; offer a concise answer and evidence links; ask a clarifying question only if it changes the recommended solution; generate a structured personal workspace; separate company facts from general background; state uncertainty clearly; never fabricate case studies, clients, integrations, certifications, pricing, SLAs or performance metrics.

The public prototype should use only synthetic documents. No personal visitor data should be retained by default. A production implementation requires privacy, security and consent review.
