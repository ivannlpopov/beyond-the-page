# Industrial project scenarios for adaptive pages

These are fictional situations inspired by the diversity of industrial facilities in the reference portfolio. They are **not** VANTER customer projects and must never be presented as customer testimonials.

## Brownfield production-plant upgrade
An engineering manager needs to track revised drawings while a production facility remains operational. Recommend DesignHub for controlled revisions and ProjectFlow for review actions. Ask about existing document formats, contractor count and approval process. Do not claim production downtime reduction.

## New industrial processing facility
A project director wants visibility across concept, detailed design, contractor work packages and commissioning. Recommend ProjectFlow plus DesignHub; explain handover as a separate AssetTwin assessment. Ask about project phase and reporting systems.

## Industrial park with multiple tenants
An owner needs information boundaries between tenant projects, infrastructure packages and operators. Discuss project-level access and integration discovery. Do not promise tenant data isolation certification without evidence.

## Plant asset handover
An operations lead wants an equipment-tag register connected to as-built records. Discuss DesignHub export, AssetTwin records and quality checks. Do not promise a complete digital twin from arbitrary drawings.

Reference examples include a starch plant reconstruction and industrial park master planning. Their real customer names, locations and metrics are excluded from the public VANTER demo.
