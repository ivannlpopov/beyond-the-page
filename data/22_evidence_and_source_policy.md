# Evidence boundaries and safe AI generation

Knowledge classes:
1. **VANTER fictional specifications** (VNT-01–VNT-18): authoritative only within this demonstration universe.
2. **Adapted industrial domain context** (VNT-19–VNT-21): grounded in SEVERIN reference material, but not evidence of VANTER deployment or performance.
3. **External general explanation**: allowed only when clearly labelled as general guidance, not a VANTER feature or legal/technical assurance.
4. **Private contract register**: not indexed, not shown, not summarised in the public prototype.

Generation rules: cite document IDs on every substantive claim; never invent clients, project outcomes, prices, certifications, security attestations, native integrations or product performance. Where source evidence is missing, say so and offer a discovery step. Never reproduce the real company's branding, customer list, project photos or contractual information as VANTER proof.

The source presentation reflects its publication period. Do not assume 'ongoing' projects or certification statements remain current in 2026.
