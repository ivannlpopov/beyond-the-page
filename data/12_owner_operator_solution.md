# Owner-operator solution

> Fictional demonstration content. Not a real vendor claim.

Industrial owner-operators need reliable information from project handover into operations. The proposed VANTER journey combines DesignHub for approved engineering records, AssetTwin for asset-centric context and Connect for transferring validated equipment data.

Example: an asset-information manager receives a handover register; checks tag completeness; links approved datasheets and commissioning evidence; then makes the resulting records searchable by maintenance teams. AssetTwin helps locate information but does not validate engineering correctness automatically.

Evaluation questions: Which tag naming convention is authoritative? What information is mandatory at handover? Who owns maintenance data? How will obsolete revisions be handled? What records may contractors access after commissioning?

No claim is made that AssetTwin replaces an enterprise asset management system or can predict failures without additional, validated capabilities.
