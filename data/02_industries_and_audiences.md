# Industries and audiences

> Fictional demonstration content. Not a real vendor claim.

VANTER targets complex, multi-stakeholder industrial programmes. In chemicals and process industries, common challenges include equipment-tag consistency, controlled revisions and handover records. In energy, teams may need cross-contractor information coordination. In advanced manufacturing, frequent design changes and commissioning dependencies can complicate execution. In infrastructure, geographically distributed suppliers and phased delivery create coordination challenges.

Typical users include project engineers, design managers, project controls teams, construction managers, asset-information managers, IT architects, procurement teams and executives. Job title alone does not establish user intent. Ask what outcome the visitor wants before assuming which product is appropriate.

Engineers generally need supported formats, revision logic, permissions and examples. IT teams need deployment, APIs, identity and security. Procurement teams need licensing scope, support, implementation dependencies and vendor evaluation. Executives need strategic fit, implementation risk and a credible evaluation approach. These are guidance patterns, not rigid personas.
